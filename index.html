Profile

Praveen Kumar is a FARE Fellow and Ph.D. Researcher in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Kanpur, India. His research interests are in the area of Autonomous Vehicle perception, dynamic SLAM, and vision-language motion planning, with a particular focus on scalability of learning and trajectory generation models (such as NeuralPathLite).

Previously, Praveen earned his M.Tech. from National Institute of Technology Rourkela (NIT Rourkela) and B.Tech. from Darbhanga College of Engineering. He has published 8+ peer-reviewed papers in prestigious venues such as IEEE T-ASE, IEEE WCCI/IJCNN, and Springer IJIRA, and was awarded the IEEE CIS Travel Grant ($1,200 USD).

His doctoral dissertation synthesized breakthroughs in vision-aided context-aware A* planning, diffusion-based trajectory generation, LLM-conditioned multi-task perception, and Vision-Text Navigation Models. He is actively seeking Postdoctoral and Assistant Professor roles in Autonomous Systems and Robotics.

Core Specializations

Research Summary & Focus Areas

Intelligent Path Planning

Designing vision-aided online A* and diffusion-based planner models (e.g., NeuralPathLite) for rapid, safe navigation of autonomous ground vehicles.

Robot Perception & SLAM

Integrating LiDAR, V-SLAM, and RGB-D multi-sensor fusion pipelines for robust object recognition and semantic mapping in real-world outdoor terrain.

LLM & Deep Learning

Pioneering Multi-Task Visual Perception Networks with Large Language Model (LLM) conditioning for context-aware autonomous navigation.

Vehicle Dynamics & Control

Neural network-based path tracking and motion control for specialized platforms such as 4WIS4WID (4-Wheel Independent Steering & Drive) electric vehicles.

Ph.D. Dissertation Highlights

Open Seminar Presentation

Ph.D. Dissertation Defense July 31, 2026 IIT Kanpur

Towards Semantically-Aware Robot Navigation: From Geometric Maps to Vision-Text Models

Presenter: Praveen Kumar  |  Thesis Advisors: Dr. Tushar Sandhan & Dr. K. R. Guruprasad (Control & Automation, Dept. of Electrical Engineering, IIT Kanpur)

This comprehensive open seminar presentation synthesizes doctoral research contributions, addressing systemic bottlenecks of metric SLAM, semantic blindness in geometric maps, and classical path planning limitations. Features key breakthroughs in vision-aided context-aware A* planning, diffusion-based trajectory generation (NeuralPathLite), LLM-conditioned multi-task perception, and Vision-Text Navigation Models (VTMMap).
